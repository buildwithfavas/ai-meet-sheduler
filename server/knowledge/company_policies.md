# Acme Corp Scheduling & Meeting Policies

## 1. Standard Working Hours
- Official working hours are **Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 (6:00 PM)** in each attendee's local timezone.
- No meetings may be scheduled to start before 09:00 or conclude after 18:00.

## 2. Weekend and Holiday Restrictions
- Meetings must **NEVER** be scheduled on Saturdays or Sundays.
- Weekend scheduling is strictly prohibited and must be rejected by the validation auditor.

## 3. Buffer Period Requirement
- A mandatory buffer period of at least **15 minutes** must exist between consecutive meetings for any participant.
- Back-to-back meetings without at least 15 minutes of recovery time are non-compliant.

## 4. Protected Focus Blocks
- **Focus Friday**: No internal meetings may be scheduled on Friday afternoons after **14:00 (2:00 PM)**.
- **Daily Lunch Block**: The period between **13:00 and 14:00 (1:00 PM - 2:00 PM)** is protected for lunch break. No meetings during this window.

## 5. Meeting Durations
- Preferred standard durations are **30 minutes** or **45 minutes**.
- Maximum single meeting duration allowed without executive escalation is **60 minutes**.

## 6. Prohibited Past Dates
- Meetings must **NEVER** be scheduled for dates that have already passed.
- All meeting dates must be on or after the current calendar date. Any attempt to schedule a meeting in the past is non-compliant and must be rejected.
